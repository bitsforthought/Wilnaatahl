# First-class Couples independent of recorded children

Wilnaatahl represents partnered relationships as first-class Couples, and links
recorded children to their parents' Couple rather than discovering relationships
only through children.
The earlier co-parent-only model could not represent a union without a recorded
child, while Couple is inclusive of marriage, common-law partnership, and other
unions without asserting which relationships count as marriages.
Each Couple has two unordered members, reflecting the symmetric treatment of
partners rather than assigning mother/father roles to the pair.

Source: [chosen design, alternatives, and scope limits](../../specs/done/childless-marriages.md#decision).
